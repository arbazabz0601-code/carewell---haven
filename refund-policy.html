Refund Policy

RP/2026/07/VOL1

We strive to provide the best service experience. This policy outlines our refund and cancellation procedures.

💵 1. Overview

At CareWell Haven Nursing Services, we are committed to providing high-quality home nursing care. We understand that sometimes situations change, and you may need to cancel or request a refund. This Refund Policy explains our procedures for cancellations, refunds, and dispute resolution.

📌 Quick Summary: We offer refunds for cancellations made with adequate notice, services not delivered, and dissatisfaction in certain cases. Equipment rental deposits are fully refundable upon return in good condition.

❌ 2. Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. Please review our cancellation policy below:

  • More than 24 hours notice: Full refund or rescheduling at no extra charge.
  • 12-24 hours notice: 50% refund of the service fee.
  • Less than 12 hours notice: No refund, but we can reschedule the appointment (subject to availability).
  • Emergency cancellations: If you need to cancel due to a medical emergency, please inform us as soon as possible. We will review your case individually and may offer a full or partial refund.

⏰ Reminder: To cancel or reschedule, please call us at +91 7669094224 or send a WhatsApp message. Email cancellations are also accepted.

🔄 3. Rescheduling Policy

  • You can reschedule your appointment free of charge if you inform us at least 4 hours before the scheduled time.
  • Rescheduling requests made less than 4 hours in advance will be treated as a cancellation.
  • We will do our best to accommodate your preferred new time and date.

💰 4. Refund Policy for Services

  • Service Not Delivered: If we are unable to deliver the service as scheduled (due to staff unavailability, technical issues, or other reasons), you will receive a 100% refund.
  • Dissatisfaction with Service: If you are not satisfied with the service provided, please inform us within 24 hours of service completion. We will investigate and may offer a partial refund or a free repeat service.
  • Partial Service: If the service is partially completed (e.g., nurse leaves early due to emergency), a prorated refund will be issued for the remaining time.
  • Long-term Packages: For long-term care packages (weekly/monthly), cancellations will be prorated based on the number of days used.

🛏️ 5. Equipment Rental Refund Policy

  • Security Deposit: The security deposit for equipment rental is fully refundable upon return of the equipment in good condition, with all accessories intact.
  • Rental Fees: Rental fees are not refundable once the equipment has been delivered and installed. However, if the equipment is returned early, a prorated refund may be issued for the unused days (subject to a 7-day minimum rental period).
  • Damaged Equipment: If the equipment is returned damaged (beyond normal wear and tear), the cost of repair or replacement will be deducted from the security deposit.
  • Early Return: If you return the equipment before the end of the rental period, we will refund the unused rental days (pro-rated), minus a 20% early termination fee.

📦 Equipment Pickup: We arrange free pickup of equipment when the rental period ends. Please ensure all accessories are returned with the equipment to avoid deductions.

💳 6. Payment & Refund Process

  • Refund Method: Refunds will be processed using the same payment method used for the original transaction.
  • Processing Time: Refunds are typically processed within 5-7 business days after approval.
  • Bank Transfers: For bank transfer refunds, please provide your account details (bank name, account number, IFSC code).
  • Cash Payments: Cash refunds will be issued in person or via bank transfer, as per your preference.

📋 7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or email.
  • Provide your booking details (name, phone number, appointment date).
  • Explain the reason for your refund request.
  • Our team will review your request and respond within 48 hours.
  • If approved, we will process the refund as per the method outlined above.

📞 Contact: For refund requests, call +91 7669094224 or email info@carewellhaven.com

⚠️ 8. Non-Refundable Items

The following items are generally non-refundable:

  • Consultation Fees: Fees for initial consultations or assessments (unless the service was not delivered).
  • Travel Charges: Travel or transportation fees charged for visits outside our service area.
  • Consumables: Used consumables (like dressings, syringes, or disposable equipment) that have been used during the service.
  • Urgent/Emergency Services: Services provided on an emergency basis are generally non-refundable.

⚖️ 9. Dispute Resolution

If you have a dispute regarding a refund or service, please follow this process:

  • Contact us directly to discuss the issue. We aim to resolve all disputes amicably.
  • If the issue is not resolved within 7 days, you may escalate the matter to our customer service manager.
  • If still unresolved, you can approach consumer courts in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, for further resolution.
