Our Services

Complete Mother & Baby Care

From pregnancy to postpartum and beyond – we're here to support every step of your parenting journey.

Our certified lactation consultants provide personalized support for:

  • Latching and positioning guidance
  • Milk supply management
  • Overcoming breastfeeding challenges
  • Pumping and storage guidance
  • Weaning support

Comprehensive care for your little one including:

  • Bathing, diapering, and grooming
  • Feeding (breast or formula)
  • Sleep routine establishment
  • Weight and health monitoring
  • Umbilical cord and circumcision care

Supporting your physical recovery after delivery:

  • C-section or episiotomy wound care
  • Perineal care and hygiene
  • Gentle postpartum exercises
  • Uterine involution monitoring
  • Nutritional guidance for recovery

Caring for your emotional well-being:

  • Postpartum depression screening
  • Emotional support and counseling
  • Stress management techniques
  • Sleep deprivation support
  • Partner and family counseling

Expert guidance on infant nutrition:

  • Breastfeeding and formula feeding support
  • Introduction to solids (when ready)
  • Allergy and intolerance identification
  • Healthy feeding routines
  • Parent education on infant nutrition

Empowering your family with knowledge:

  • Newborn care workshops for parents
  • Baby safety and first-aid training
  • Sibling adjustment support
  • Sleep training guidance
  • Postnatal planning and support
