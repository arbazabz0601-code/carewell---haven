Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about physiotherapy at home.

Physiotherapy at home brings professional rehabilitation to your doorstep. A certified physiotherapist visits your home with portable equipment to provide exercises, manual therapy, and pain relief techniques – eliminating the need to travel to a clinic.
While a referral is not mandatory, we recommend consulting your doctor. We also work closely with your physician to ensure coordinated care and safe, effective treatment aligned with your medical history.
Each session typically lasts 45-60 minutes. The duration may vary depending on your condition, treatment goals, and the specific exercises or therapies involved.
Physiotherapy treats a wide range of conditions including back and neck pain, joint injuries, arthritis, stroke recovery, sports injuries, post-surgery rehab, neurological conditions, and chronic pain.
